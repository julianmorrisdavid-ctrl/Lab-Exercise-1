My First time playing tennis

I tried tennis for the first time last weekend and it was way harder than it looks on TV. My serve kept hitting the net, and I didn’t realize how much running is involved just to return one shot. After a few games I started getting the hang of the swing, though my arm was sore the next day. I’m definitely going to keep practicing because it’s actually really fun once you get past the awkward first attempts.


Getting used to playing tennis

After my first rough game, I’ve been playing tennis every weekend to get better. My serve is still inconsistent, but my footwork has improved a lot and I don’t get tired as fast anymore. It’s slowly starting to feel more natural instead of just running around trying to hit the ball.
